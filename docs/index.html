Why Resonance Breathing?

Your cardiovascular system possesses an intrinsic resonance frequency governed by the baroreflex loop. One generic cadence does not fit all bodies.

🫀

0° Phase Synchrony

When breathing at your resonant cadence (~0.1 Hz / 4.5–6.5 BPM), inhalation-driven tachycardia and exhalation-driven bradycardia align in exact synchrony, dramatically amplifying vagal tone.

Vascular transit delay correlates directly with arterial length. Peak Frequency auto-calculates your personalized starting anchor using your height and biological sex from your Garmin user profile.

The Lehrer 2-Round Calibration Protocol

How Peak Frequency systematically discovers your precise physiological resonance cadence in under 12 minutes.

00

Rest Baseline

2 minutes of spontaneous natural breathing. Establishes baseline resting heart rate and baseline HRV metrics without guidance.

01

Round 1: Coarse Scan

Tests 3 cadences (Center, +0.25 BPM, -0.25 BPM) with 1-minute settling and 2-minute recording legs to detect peak HRV delta.

02

Round 2: Fine Tuning

Narrows around Round 1's winning frequency with micro-increments of ±0.125 BPM to isolate your definitive resonance peak.

03

Resonance Lock

The optimal cadence locks permanently into watch memory for daily 40/60 resonance training, accompanied by live synchrony graphs.
